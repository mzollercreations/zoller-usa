The Indispensable All-Around Tool for Turning Operations

Whether you want to adjust and measure tools for lathes, machining centers, or milling centers—with ZOLLER »hyperion,« a single measuring machine is all you need for virtually all of your different tool holders. The various tool holders are fixed on a turret, making them especially quick and simple to use.

Whether you choose manual or CNC-controlled operation, with this device you’re opting for maximum versatility and can immediately benefit from a significant increase in productivity thanks to tools that are 100% prepared and inspected.

Flexible turret configuration tailored to your needs

A single measuring machine for almost all different types of tool holders

Specialized alloy and robust construction

Starting with a batch size of 1

20% increase in productivity

The universal presetting and measuring machines in ZOLLER’s »hyperion« series guarantee maximum efficiency and flexibility when holding tools horizontally in CNC manufacturing. Tools from virtually all tooling systems are measured and adjusted quickly, simply, and reliably. This means less scrap and machine downtime, higher quality, greater productivity—and higher profits.

In short: With ZOLLER »hyperion«, you can manufacture cost-effectively, on time, profitably, and with perfect quality.

With »the »hyperion« 300/500/700«, you’ll have a measuring machine suitable for virtually all tooling systems, giving you a decisive edge over the competition.
