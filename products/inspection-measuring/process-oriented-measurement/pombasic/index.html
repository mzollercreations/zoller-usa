»pomBasic«

Process-Oriented Compact Solutions
for Tool Inspection

Request Information Now

View in the 3D showroom

We stand for smart progress

Would you like to speed up your work processes? Then take the abbreviation when it comes to tool measurement and inspection. The ZOLLER »pomBasic« series is the ideal solution for a well-designed, cost-effective setup and manufacturing of precision tools.

Save yourself unnecessary trips to the measuring room—benefit from efficient processes and precise results in record time. With their compact dimensions, ZOLLER’s »pomBasic« inspection units can be positioned anywhere in the production area—for example, right next to the CNC grinding machine, in the goods receipt area, or at the tool dispensing station.

Request a 1:1 expert consultation now

Calibrating measuring machines on-site using certified test specimens and reference standards is an important part of E. ZOLLER GmbH & Co. KG’s quality assurance process—this ensures reliable measurement results and the high precision of your products in accordance with applicable standards.

Automatic Inspection Using the Macro Function

Do you have repetitive measuring tasks and want to perform them effectively? ZOLLER has the perfect solution for this, too: Thanks to the macro function, repeat measurements can be saved simply and reliably.

A single click on your saved macro program is all it takes, and the measurement process runs automatically: simple, fast, and user-friendly.

2. Positioning the tool to be measured relative to the reference image

Measuring Saw Blades Tooth by Tooth

The saw blade holder expands the range of applications for »pomBasic«. It allows saw blades with diameters ranging from 150 mm to 600 mm to be clamped using reducer rings that correspond to them.
