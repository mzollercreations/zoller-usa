Invitation

Your invitation to Torrance

What awaits you in Torrance – and why it’s worth the visit.

Three solutions. One automated tool cycle.

From the tool store via preparation to the CNC machine: see how ZOLLER automation works hand in hand – and what it means for your production.

»coraLogistic«

Automated shop floor logistics

  • Tools travel on their own: the autonomous »coraExpress« robot transports them between storage, preparation and machine
  • The »smartPallet« system holds tools securely – for manual or automated transport
  • Create transport orders in just a few clicks in webTMS job management
  • Less walking and searching – the tool arrives at the machine on time
View product

»coraVarion«

Automated assembly, inspection and measurement of indexable inserts

  • With »micBox«: indexable inserts are turned, flipped and changed fully automatically
  • Assembly, inspection and measurement in one continuous process
  • Consistent quality with complete documentation of every tool
  • Runs unmanned – your skilled staff can focus on value-adding tasks
View product

»loadBox«

The intelligent tool center

  • Picks tools to order and provides them for logistics
  • A FANUC 6-axis robot reliably handles tools between input/output and storage
  • Compact tool storage in the Kardex Shuttle lift system
  • Server-based control – operated via monitor, tablet or smartphone
View product
